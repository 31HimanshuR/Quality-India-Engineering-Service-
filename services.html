Pile Foundation Survey

Advanced testing and analysis services to ensure structural integrity, load capacity, and safety of foundation systems.

Pile Integrity Test

Evaluates pile quality and detects defects using low strain impact methods.

Cross-Hole Sonic Logging

Ultrasonic testing to assess concrete quality and detect voids or cracks.

Pile Load Test (Kentledge Method)

Load testing using static weights to determine pile bearing capacity.

Pile Load Test (Anchor Method)

Reaction-based testing using anchor piles for controlled load application.

Pile Load Test (Rock Anchor Method)

High-capacity load testing using rock anchors for deep foundation analysis.

NDT (Non-Destructive Testing)

Comprehensive testing solutions to assess structural integrity, strength, and durability without causing damage to the structure.

UPV Test

Ultrasonic Pulse Velocity test to evaluate concrete quality and uniformity.

Rebound Hammer Test

Surface hardness testing to estimate compressive strength of concrete.

Carbonation Test

Determines carbonation depth affecting reinforcement durability.

Core Cutting

Extracting concrete samples for laboratory strength analysis.

Half Cell Potential Test

Measures corrosion probability of reinforcing steel in concrete.

Rebar Locating Test

Detects position and depth of reinforcement bars within concrete.

Mortar Test

Evaluates bonding strength and material performance of mortar.
