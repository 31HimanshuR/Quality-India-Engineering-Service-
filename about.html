Who We Are

Quality India Engineering Services (QIES) is a multidisciplinary consulting firm delivering advanced solutions in engineering surveys, remote sensing, and structural analysis.

With a strong focus on accuracy and innovation, we specialize in Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR), Drone Surveys, LiDAR Mapping, Non-Destructive Testing (NDT), and GIS-based solutions.

Our team of experienced engineers and technical experts ensures reliable data and high-quality results for infrastructure, construction, and development projects across India.
